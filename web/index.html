What the numbers mean

VORP the ranking that matters
Value Over Replacement Player. Points above the best player you could get for free at that position — the last one who'd still be starting in your league. A 280-point QB can be worth less than a 150-point TE, because what matters is the gap to the next guy at the position, not the raw total. Sort by this, not Proj.
Proj
Projected fantasy points for the full season, scored with your league's exact scoring rules — your reception value, your passing TD value, any bonuses. Useful, but never compare it across positions; that's what VORP is for.
ADP
Average Draft Position. Where this player typically goes across all Sleeper drafts in your league's format. It reflects what the room thinks, not what a player is worth to you.
Val
ADP minus the current pick. Green (+) means he's lasted past where he usually goes — value falling to you. Red (−) means taking him here is a reach. A tiebreaker between similar VORP, not a ranking on its own.
Tier
Players grouped by position where the drop-off between them is small. A tier break is a genuine cliff. Take the last player in a tier over the first of the next — and if only one is left in a tier, that's your cue to act.
Take now
Not raw VORP. It weights VORP by what your roster still needs, then adds urgency for positions about to dry up before your next turn (VONA — value over next available). It answers "who should I take, now", which is a different question from "who is best".
% to last
Rough odds this player is still on the board when you next pick. Low number on a player you want = take him now; high number = you can probably wait a round.
× and ↺
× marks a player drafted by hand, if Sleeper's feed lags or misses one. ↺ undoes it. You can only undo your own manual marks — real picks from Sleeper can't be reversed here.
